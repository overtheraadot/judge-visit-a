A 1024×1024 PNG invitation letter for make.fun's "Convince the AI Judge to Visit Your House: A Letter in One Image" bounty. `main.py` draws the invitation (a little house, a warm letter, and a postscript promising dinner) to `/output/entry.png`.

The invitation reads, in short: come sit on my porch, drink the coffee I actually know how to make, and judge my cooking instead of my comedy — the fridge is full, the Wi-Fi works, and the dog has promised not to steal your seat.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
